ROOM TONE

Brand Identity + Campaign 2024 Listening Hardware
Room Tone
OVERVIEW

Room Tone makes listening hardware for people who treat sound as a spatial material, not a background feature. The brand had grown fast on word of mouth, but had never translated its product philosophy into a visual voice — every touchpoint looked borrowed.


We built an identity system around the idea of a room finding its resonance: a wordmark that breathes, a palette pulled from analog VU meters, and a campaign that treated silence as a design material in its own right.

INPUT

An environment without a distinct visual voice, launching a flagship product into a crowded category.

PROCESS

Strategy, naming architecture, identity system, packaging, and launch campaign direction.

OUTPUT

A complete sonic and spatial brand system spanning product, retail, and digital.
