VERTEX

Campaign Concept 2023 Architecture Studio
Vertex
OVERVIEW

Vertex is a self-initiated campaign concept exploring how an architecture studio might announce a single, monumental structure — a building defined less by its footprint than by the point at which its lines converge.


The concept treats negative space as the hero: every frame is built around a vanishing point, every headline collapses toward a single word. It was developed as a speculative pitch and never produced client-side, but set the visual language later reused across two built campaigns.

INPUT

An open brief: propose a launch campaign for a single architectural landmark.

PROCESS

Concept development, art direction, spatial visualization, campaign key art.

OUTPUT

A speculative campaign system built around convergence, scale, and restraint.
