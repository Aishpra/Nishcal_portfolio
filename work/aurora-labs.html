AURORA LABS

Visual System + Website 2025 Research & Innovation
Aurora Labs
OVERVIEW

Aurora Labs runs long-horizon research programs across materials science and applied physics. The work is genuinely difficult to explain to a general audience, and the existing site buried it under academic convention — dense text, no visual hierarchy, nothing to hold onto.


We designed a visual system built around a single motif — concentric fields of distortion, standing in for the labs' own research into gravitational and electromagnetic systems — and used it to structure a website that makes six research tracks legible at a glance.

INPUT

Six research programs, no shared visual language, a website that read as a document dump.

PROCESS

Visual system design, information architecture, website design and build, motion direction.

OUTPUT

A generative visual language and a website that turns complexity into an asset.
